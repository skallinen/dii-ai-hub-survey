How the triangles work

You are not picking one corner. You are weighing all three against each other and placing your dot where the balance falls for your moment.

  • Near a corner: that one dominated.
  • On an edge between two: those two mattered, the third barely.
  • In the middle: all three pulled about equally.

If a placement feels hard, that is the point. The difficulty is you actually weighing the tradeoff, which is exactly what we want to see. There is no right answer and no good corner; all three are real.
